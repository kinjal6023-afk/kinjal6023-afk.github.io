About Me

Meet Kinjal.

I help organizations make confident, data-informed decisions through well-designed dashboards, analytical models, and clearly framed business questions.

I hold an MBA in Business Analytics (STEM-designated) from Saint Peter's University, with 3+ years of combined experience in business and operations analysis, compliance workflow design, and BI reporting. My work centers on converting stakeholder requirements into reliable SQL and Power BI solutions.

Previously, at Vinayak Consultancy, I designed a compliance review workflow that improved case-approval accuracy by 20% and established audit-ready processes in a regulated environment.

I am proficient in SQL, Python, and DAX, and I am most motivated by turning complex data into clear, actionable insight.
